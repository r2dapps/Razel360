360° Visualizer Render Specs

Give these exact instructions to your 3D architectural rendering artists

1. Aspect Ratio & Dimensions (Strict 2:1)

All renders MUST be rendered with a strict 2:1 aspect ratio using an Equirectangular (Spherical 360°) Camera. Recommended resolutions:

  • Production Standard: 4096 × 2048 px (Sharp, fast mobile loading)
  • Ultra 8K Luxury: 6144 × 3072 px or 8192 × 4096 px

2. Camera Height & Level Horizon

Camera height must strictly mimic natural human standing eye-level: 1.55m to 1.65m (5.1 – 5.4 ft) above the finished floor level. Keep camera Pitch and Roll perfectly at 0 degrees to prevent dizzying tilts.

3. Color Space & Lighting Continuity

Export in sRGB color profile (PNG or high-quality JPEG). Ensure window lighting and exterior time-of-day match across adjacent rooms (e.g. living room, kitchen, and balcony share identical golden hour sky illumination).
